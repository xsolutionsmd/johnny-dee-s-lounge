## Tone

The indexed official site uses the category phrase “American Bar & Grill Restaurant.” **Historical official wording.** [S4]

Customer accounts suggest a familiar neighborhood atmosphere and friendly service. **Third-party perceptions, including older reviews.** [S1][S2]

**Inferred writing direction:** warm, straightforward and local; make food selection, location and calling easy to understand. This is a recommendation, not an owner-approved voice.
