The lounge

A familiar room on Amuskai Road

Johnny Dee’s Lounge is a neighborhood American bar & grill in Parkville, a relaxed place for a sandwich, a plate from the grill or a drink with friends.

The kitchen turns out shrimp salad, homemade soups and specials alongside bar & grill classics. For bigger occasions, the lounge also has a catering hall.
